# License decisions pending author approval

No external paper full texts or font files are redistributed. The supplied Phase 1 audit is retained as user-provided research provenance. Scholarly works are linked and cited, not republished.

A permissive software license such as MIT is a candidate for original library code; a separate attribution license such as CC BY 4.0 is a candidate for the manuscript and original figures. These are options, not an assertion that licensing has already been approved or that every bundled item is covered. The human rightsholder must confirm ownership, attribution requirements, dependency notices and the intended licenses before publication.

The package deliberately does not invent a copyright holder, assign a license to third-party material, claim a public release, or represent DOI issuance as peer review. Record final license identifiers separately for code, manuscript and data when approved.
