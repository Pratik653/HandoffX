# Document quality assurance

The final DOCX was rendered using LibreOffice through the prescribed document renderer. The delivered PDF is that final render. The manuscript has **58 pages**, **6 figures** and **11 tables**; the executive summary has **2 pages**. All pages were visually inspected, with changed pages checked again after corrections. All 19 contents entries match their physical PDF pages. Figure and table lists use verified page references. PDF text and image bounds have no out-of-page elements in the automated check.

An early native-equation conversion failed visibly: an expression lost its state vector and some equation labels were malformed. Those intermediate files are not delivered. Every mathematical expression was then rendered by LaTeX and embedded at high resolution with its LaTeX source as alternative text. The canonical Markdown retains editable LaTeX. There are 211 mathematical occurrences (178 unique expressions). These are **not native editable Word equation objects**. Ordinary DOCX text, headings and tables remain editable. The manuscript and complete AI document preserve the same substantive material; their navigation differs appropriately by format.

Layout uses Letter portrait, readable Times New Roman body text, restrained headings, captions, repeated table headers and page numbering. APA author-date/reference conventions are applied to a monograph layout, not a strict double-spaced student-paper template. External APA/editorial certification has not been obtained. Author information is explicitly pending owner confirmation; it is not an invented affiliation.

The manuscript was kept at the length supported by the executed study rather than padded to the requested conditional 120-150-page target. No diagram substitutes for a computed figure, and the six result figures are generated from the stored data. `DOCUMENT_VALIDATION.json` records structural and citation checks. Full research content appears in `01_Publication/HandoffX_Research_Paper.md` and `06_AI_Knowledge_Base/COMPLETE_RESEARCH_FOR_AI.md`.
