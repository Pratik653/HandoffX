# Updated novelty audit

## Decision: Category C, retained with stronger qualifications

The supplied audit selected comparative empirical/software research. Current execution adds a bounded implementation and synthetic findings, not a new optimal-stopping or robust-POMDP theory. The explicit Bayes-adaptive POMDP correspondence (Ross et al., 2011) closes any remaining claim that jointly tracking unknown capability and hidden task conditions is itself novel.

## What the package contributes

A transparent finite model; typed probability interfaces; four action choices; nine-model joint prior; a separate rectangular robust policy; a matched oracle; exact and Monte Carlo evaluation; scalar trajectory cross-checks; documented source corrections; reproducible data and negative results. These components are newly assembled here, but their mathematical principles are established.

## Abandoned claims and restrictions

Do not call a model-conditional robust value a safety certificate outside its uncertainty set. Do not equate an iid-mode ablation with a universally naive baseline. Do not label the static-loss baseline a reproduction of neural learning-to-defer. Do not present the prior as a fitted posterior. Do not call the Linux timing a Windows laptop benchmark. Do not convert synthetic counterpart outcomes into real causal evidence. Do not present local protocol recording as independent public preregistration.

## Research recommendation after execution

Proceed with the research software as a transparent reference benchmark, not a marketed novel controller. The highest-value next evidence is a prospectively specified, low-risk real-agent calibration study with ethically obtained human-rescue outcomes and an explicit intervention-data collection policy. Benchmark generality, probability estimation and deployment relevance need substantially more work than adding further Bellman algebra.
